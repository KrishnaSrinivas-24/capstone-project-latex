\begin{frame}{Literature Review: Selection Strategy}
\begin{block}{Review-I requirement}
The GITAM template asks for \textbf{4--6 full-length papers}, one detailed slide per paper for at least four papers, followed by a comparison table.
\end{block}
\begin{itemize}
\item Six papers form the core research set for this review.
\item They span memory lifecycle, shared state, architectural framing, governance and conflict handling.
\item The review supports the identification of research gaps rather than a generic bibliography list.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
